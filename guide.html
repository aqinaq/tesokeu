GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Where are my books saved?

Your imported texts, bookmarks, reading position, preferences, activity, and weekly goal are saved in this browser. If you connect devices, a copy is stored online so both browsers can sync. Download a backup from the Bookshelf page to keep your own copy.

How do I connect my phone?

On your laptop, open Bookshelf and choose “Make a device code.” On your phone, open the same site, go to Bookshelf, and enter the code. It works once and expires after 10 minutes. The phone's current bookshelf is replaced when you connect. Afterward, changes sync while the site is open on each device.

Can I stop syncing?

Use “Disconnect this browser” to keep its books here and stop syncing it. “Delete synced data” removes the online collection for all devices; copies already saved in each browser remain there.

How do I find a place in a long read?

Use the position slider beneath the reader to preview and jump through the text, or enter an exact word number in “Go to word.” Save a bookmark with the star button, then revisit it from the reader or Bookshelf.

Can I hide the rest of the page while reading?

Use the expand button beside the reading title to open focus view. Press Escape or use the close button to leave it.

What files can I upload?

Tesokeu accepts PDF and EPUB files up to 12 MB, plus TXT files up to 2 MB. You can also paste text or add a public article link.

Should I always read as fast as possible?

No. Choose a speed that lets you follow and enjoy the material. Slower settings may work better for unfamiliar or complex writing.

What does WPM mean?

WPM stands for words per minute. At 300 WPM, a word normally appears for about 200 milliseconds; punctuation pauses can make some words stay longer.
